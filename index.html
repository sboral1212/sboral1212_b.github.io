Instructions for Presenters

  1. Use the screen-sharing option at the bottom of the Zoom window to share your presentation.
  2. Share your entire screen to reduce potential technical difficulties.
  3. The Zoom floating window can be hidden using Ctrl + Alt + Shift + H.
  4. Keep your camera switched on during the presentation to support an engaging discussion.
  5. Sessions may be recorded. Presenters who do not wish to be recorded should inform the organisers before their presentation.
